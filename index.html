Door frame cut planner

Pick the stock you have, enter the customer’s cuts, and get a cut plan for each piece.

1. Stock on hand

Other size (feet):

Leave “On hand” blank if you have plenty of that size. Tick “Use first” on short or odd lengths you want to clear: the plan fills those before touching regular stock. Sizes you add yourself are ticked automatically.

2. Customer’s cuts (inches)

Enter lengths in inches without symbols: 32 = 2. One per line or all in a row. Use 5' only when entering feet. The planner searches selected stock lengths for the best overall combination: fewer stock pieces, less total stock length, then less leftover.
